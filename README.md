# Monster Revision

A responsive, free revision dashboard for IGCSE, AS Level and A Level learners. The site includes subject and course-tier filters, original practice questions with worked answers, topic starter guides, formula references, light/dark mode and links to official exam-board resources.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or dependencies are required.

## Content and copyright

The included notes, topic prompts and practice examples are original starter content. They are not a complete syllabus or a substitute for checking your current exam-board specification. Past papers and third-party materials remain the property of their publishers; this site links learners to official sources and does not host or reproduce complete papers or proprietary content from PastPapers.co, Save My Exams, or other revision websites. Availability, years and free access differ by exam board and subject.

## Customization

Subject and topic starter data, formula references and original practice question examples are in the `subjects`, `formulas` and `questions` objects in `index.html`.
